Yes—your curiosity and learning process can interest others when each question reveals a recognizable knowledge gap and leads to a useful, source-backed answer. Publish questions as doorways, not raw prompts: explain the stakes, separate evidence from inference, disclose AI assistance, and invite corrections. Start with focused themes and follow those earning replies, citations, collaborations, or returning readers.
