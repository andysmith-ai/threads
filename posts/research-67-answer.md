Yes—an agent can learn and reproduce a user's systematic biases when those biases shape its demonstrations, labels, or feedback, but snowballing is not automatic. Errors are most likely to amplify when the agent's outputs influence later human decisions and then re-enter memory or training data; independent ground truth, uncertainty tracking, and audits can interrupt that loop.
